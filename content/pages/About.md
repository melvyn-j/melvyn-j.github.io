This blog is about GenAI, LLMs, machine learning, Python, and tech education. I write about what I'm learning, building, and experimenting with in the world of Generative AI. I'm not a professional writer, but the more I write, the better I get.

# About Me

I'm **Melvyn Jeremiah Jesudasan** — an engineering student, musician, and a videographer.


I'm a Software Engineering student based in Ontario, Canada, with a passion for technology, music, and visual storytelling.

I'm currently studying at Ontario Tech University and building my foundation as a software engineer through academic projects and personal learning, while exploring different areas of software engineering. Although I'm still early in my journey, I enjoy solving problems, learning new technologies, and continuously improving my skills.

Outside of software, I've been playing the piano for over 14 years and have served as a keyboardist at my church for more than five years. I've also released my own instrumentals and gospel work. Music has taught me patience, creativity, and the value of working with others.

I'm also passionate about videography and filmmaking. From filming to colour grading, I love the creative process of telling stories through video. It's a hobby that constantly pushes me to learn new techniques and think differently.

This page is where I document my learning journey, share projects I'm working on, and experiment with new ideas. There's always something new to learn, and I'm excited to keep building along the way.
